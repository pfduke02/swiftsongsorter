How many battles

You can stop at any point and see the ranking inferred from the battles so far. The first 40 battles learn your album and style taste; after that the sorter concentrates on pinning down your top 26, then your top 52, then the rest. A full sort of 250 songs would take about 1,800 battles; 500 gets the top of the list mostly right.

Progress saves in this browser and online, so you can close the tab and come back. You get a share link for your ranking.
